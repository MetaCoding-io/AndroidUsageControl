# S04: Apps and Modes v1 — UAT

**Milestone:** M007
**Written:** 2026-11-04T06:57:34.588Z

## UAT Type
- UAT mode: runtime-executable

## Preconditions
Debug APK installed. Notification access granted. Some notification history exists. DND / notification-policy access is granted for the ringer test.

## Steps
1. Open the Apps tab. Expected: the apps_list shows rows sorted by alerts/day descending, each with an icon, label, 7-day sparkline and "you dismiss N%" hint.
2. Tap a row. Expected: app_profile opens with the ring, median time to act, hourly bars, channels (NULL shows as "Unknown channel"), today's screen time, the limit control and suggestions.
3. Set a daily limit, leave the profile and reopen it. Expected: the limit persists and no duplicate rule is created.
4. Open the Modes tab. Expected: modes_list shows Focus, Sleep and Normal cards, the context strip and a screen-time summary.
5. Toggle Sleep or Focus. Expected: the ringer mode changes, and `adb logcat -s SoundProfileApplier` shows the preset id with the outcome applied.

## Edge cases
- Revoke notification-policy access, then toggle a mode. Expected: a snackbar appears and logcat shows permission-denied.
- An app with no actions shows the median as an em dash or empty, not a crash.

## Operational Readiness
Health signal: `SoundProfileApplier` logcat lines with outcome applied, and the ringer state matching the chosen preset. Failure signal: permission-denied or error outcomes in logcat, plus the Modes snackbar. Recovery: grant notification-policy access in system settings and toggle again. Monitoring gaps: there is no remote telemetry, and auto presets can silently override a manual pick.
