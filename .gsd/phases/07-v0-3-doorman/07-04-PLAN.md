# S04: Apps and Modes v1

**Milestone:** M007
**Slice:** S04

**Goal:** Replace the Apps and Modes placeholders with real screens: an Apps list sorted by alerts/day with sparklines and a dismiss hint, an app profile (ring, median time to act, hourly bars, channels, screen-time limit, suggestions), and a Modes tab of preset cards whose toggle applies the sound profile through a core-common bridge.
**Demo:** Apps lists apps by alerts/day with sparklines; app profile shows stats, channels, limit, suggestions; Modes shows preset cards whose toggle applies the sound profile

## Must-Haves

- `./gradlew assembleDebug check :app:assembleDebugAndroidTest` exits 0
- JVM AppStats tests pass, covering median (empty, odd and even counts), local-day bucketing across midnight, a 24-entry hourly histogram, dismiss rate, and NULL channel grouped as Unknown
- testTags apps_list, app_profile, modes_list and mode_toggle_ exist in the sources
- PlaceholderScreens.kt is removed and MainActivity routes APPS, MODES and apps/{packageName} to the real screens
- Feature modules gain no dependency on service modules (SoundProfileApplier bridge lives in core-common)
- On-device UAT: toggling a mode changes the ringer mode

## Proof Level

- This slice proves: integration: compiled wiring plus JVM tests; real runtime and human UAT are required for the ringer change

## Integration Closure

Consumes the v4 notifications DAO, UsageDao, SuggestionDao, RuleDao, SoundPresetDao and SoundProfileExecutor. New wiring: Apps, Modes and profile nav routes in MainActivity, and a SoundProfileApplier Hilt binding in service-context. After this, the milestone needs only on-device UAT.

## Verification

- SoundProfileApplierImpl logs under logcat tag SoundProfileApplier (preset id plus outcome: applied, permission-denied or error) and returns a sealed result that the Modes UI surfaces as a snackbar.
