# S03: Shell and Now

**Milestone:** M007
**Slice:** S03

**Goal:** Replace the five-tab shell with Now/Apps/Modes plus a Settings gear, and make Now the daily stream: real v4 notification rows with icons and lane dots, time sections and bundles, a hero count, a context strip, search, a recently dismissed list, swipe-to-archive with 10 s undo, swipe-left snooze to a clock time, and tap to open the original (R012).
**Demo:** Bottom bar Now/Apps/Modes; Now shows real notifications with icons and lane dots, swipe archive with undo, swipe-left snooze to time, tap opens original

## Must-Haves

- `./gradlew assembleDebug check` exits 0.
- JVM tests for NowStream grouping pass in `:feature:feature-notifications:testDebugUnitTest`: sections, bundles, ProvisionalLane→Lane mapping, hero count, and search filter.
- `:app:assembleDebugAndroidTest` compiles the instrumented DAO tests for the new queries.
- Bottom bar shows exactly Now/Apps/Modes (testTags nav_tab_now/apps/modes), and a gear opens settings.
- Archive and snooze go through the core NotificationActions interface. A null listener produces a visible error, never a crash.
- Undo inside 10 s does not cancel the system notification.
- On device (UAT): an adb-posted notification shows up in Now with an icon and lane dot, and the swipe, snooze and tap behaviours work as described.

## Proof Level

- This slice proves: integration. Real runtime is required. On-device swipe, snooze and open are checked in UAT.

## Integration Closure

Consumes the S01 core-ui components and LaneColors, plus S02 NotificationDao v4, IconStore paths, PendingIntentCache and NotificationCaptureService. Adds the NotificationActions interface in core-common, implemented in service-notification and bound with Hilt. Remaining for the milestone: the S04 Apps/Modes content.

## Verification

- The NotificationCapture logcat tag logs archive, snooze and open actions with key and outcome: cancelled, snoozed, listener-unavailable, or intent-missing→launch-fallback.
